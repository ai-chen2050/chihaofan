FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Details on meal planning logic, privacy storage, multi-platform, and subscriptions

How does Chihaofan plan meals in 3 seconds?

Chihaofan uses a large language model reasoning engine with three transparent constraints: 1. Inventory Priority (prioritizing food labeled 'expiring soon' to eliminate waste); 2. Budget constraints; 3. Dietary preferences and allergen avoidance. It generates balanced dishes and pushes missing ingredients into an auto shopping list in 3 seconds.

Why is Chihaofan privacy-first?

Chihaofan adheres to a pure Zero-Server Architecture. We operate no centralized backend databases to store your food inventory, spending records, or personal dietary profiles. All data is kept in your device sandbox and syncs only through your private Apple iCloud account.

What is the difference in the Huawei AppGallery version?

The Huawei AppGallery build currently excludes generative AI features (AI meal planning, Qingtuan chat, receipt OCR, health reports) due to ongoing domestic algorithm regulatory filings. All other core capabilities (3D fridge, recipes, shopping lists, expense tracking, and pet companion) match the iOS build identically.

Can I bring my own API key (BYOK)?

Yes. Chihaofan supports Bring-Your-Own-Key mode. You can enter your own OpenAI / DeepSeek / compatible API endpoint and key in Settings. Requests are sent directly from your phone to your AI provider with no middleman proxy.

Will my data be lost if I change or reset my device?

Because we do not store your data on our servers, make sure to enable iCloud Backup in the app settings, or export an offline JSON backup before resetting your phone. You can restore your data with one tap on your new device.

Your Meals & Grocery Data Belong Strictly to You

Chihaofan is a native, local-first offline utility with zero developer-owned servers. Your ingredients, spending history, and family dietary profiles remain exclusively on your phone.
